Hare Run — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-05
App: Hare Run (com.harerun.app)
Developer: Bunvaro Games
Contact: bunvarogames@gmail.com

1. Summary

Hare Run is a single-player arcade chase game with an optional online leaderboard. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game and the leaderboard. We do not collect your real name, email, phone number, precise location, photos, contacts, or an advertising identifier. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.

This policy is kept consistent with our Google Play Data Safety disclosure: analytics is off until you consent, crash/diagnostic data runs for stability, and the leaderboard stores your nickname and score.

2. What we collect and why

  • Pseudonymous account identifier (user ID). When you first open the game, Firebase Anonymous Authentication creates a random pseudonymous identifier. This is considered personal data under the GDPR even though it is not your name, email, or any directly identifying detail — and we treat it accordingly. Purpose: to own your leaderboard entry so only you can update it.
  • Nickname. If you submit a score to the global leaderboard, the nickname you choose is stored and shown publicly to other players. Purpose: to label your leaderboard entry.
  • Game scores and progress. Your score, wave reached, and game mode are stored with your leaderboard entry. Your personal best runs and unlock progress are also stored locally on your device. Purpose: leaderboard ranking and game progression.
  • Gameplay analytics — off unless you say yes. The first time you open the game we ask, on a screen with two equally weighted choices, whether we may record how you play. Nothing is recorded unless you choose “Allow” — collection is switched off before the game starts and is only switched on by your answer. If you consent, we record a small set of events: the game being opened, a run started, a run ended (mode, biome, score, level or wave reached, pups gathered, how long the field took), an unlock earned, and a generic flag if the game crashes. These events never include your name or any free-form text you type. When analytics is enabled, Firebase may also process limited device/session identifiers needed to provide the service. Purpose: to see where the game is too hard, too slow or not fun. Legal basis: your consent. You can withdraw consent at any time from the pause menu (“Share play data”), which switches collection back off immediately. Declining, or later withdrawing, changes nothing else about the game — every mode and the global leaderboard work exactly the same either way.
  • Crash & diagnostic data — always on. To keep the game stable we use Firebase Crashlytics. When the app crashes it collects a crash report (error type, stack trace, device state) and a pseudonymous installation identifier. This contains no name, email, or free-form text, and is never used for advertising or behavioural tracking. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable and secure.

What we do NOT collect

Real name, email address, phone number, precise or coarse location, photos or media, contacts, microphone/camera input, advertising ID, or your device’s contact list. (The game also does not currently upload gameplay replays.)

3. How data is shared

We use Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, and Crashlytics) as a data processor. We configure Firebase to respect consent settings: analytics data collection is controlled at runtime and stays off until you consent, while crash/diagnostic reporting runs under our legitimate interest in stability. Data is processed under Google’s privacy terms: firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy. Your nickname and score are visible to other players on the public leaderboard. We do not share data with any third party for advertising or marketing.

4. Data security and retention

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Your leaderboard entry (nickname + score) is retained until you request deletion. Local data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

5. Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Analytics. You are asked once, on first launch, and nothing is recorded unless you agree. You can change that answer at any time from the pause menu (“Share play data”). Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent and takes effect immediately.
  • Your leaderboard entry. You can request deletion of your entry (nickname and score) by emailing the address at the top of this policy. Because nicknames are not guaranteed unique, please include your nickname, your approximate score, and the approximate date you set that score, so we can identify the right entry if more than one player has used the same nickname. There is no in-app self-delete yet, so email is the way to do it.
  • Local data. Everything stored on your device — best scores, progress, unlocks, your consent answer — is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data (e.g. GDPR / CCPA); contact us at the address above to exercise them.

6. Children’s privacy

Hare Run is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal details such as a child’s name, email, or contact information: there is no sign-up and no personal details are requested (accounts are pseudonymous). A parent or guardian who believes a child’s nickname should be removed, or who wants their data deleted, can contact us at the address above.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it — for example if we add advertising — we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top before the change takes effect.

8. Contact

Questions or requests: bunvarogames@gmail.com.
